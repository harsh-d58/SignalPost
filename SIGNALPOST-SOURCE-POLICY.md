# Signalpost source policy

This records the source policy supplied in chat and the organizer's publication feedback. For the exact endpoints, licences, retention rules, and access patterns implemented by this repository, read [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

Official records establish the legal entity. The website, brand, and accounts require separate ownership evidence.

## Publication rules

- Resolve the domain, page, or profile to the exact legal entity before attributing facts. Label parent, group, subsidiary, franchise, manager, and public-brand relationships explicitly. For a group-hosted candidate, find the exact legal name or organisation number in ownership context and limit claims to the verified entity scope.
- Verify social handles individually. Parent, theme, and agency footer links can appear on otherwise valid sites. A candidate handle needs support linking the account to the exact company.
- Publish news from real dated detail posts with substantive content. Menu labels, product pages, and update/modified dates alone do not support a news claim or publication date.
- Publish hiring from individual active ads for the exact company or verified subunit. Careers landing pages, NAV search links, and a parent's vacancies do not qualify as evidence for a specific opening.
- Record the source URL or durable identifier, retrieval time, effective or reporting date where relevant, content hash, and extraction method for every claim. Retain a supporting quote or locator and relevant entity/account scope. Use explicit missing, blocked, and ambiguous states. A company's promotional copy can support an attributed business description. Independent sentiment requires an independent source.

## Restricted platforms and unofficial crawlers

Use access permitted by the source's terms and applicable law. Check permissions for collection, retention, and publication along with robots guidance and outbound URL safety. A public page or an available GitHub client does not establish authorization. LinkedIn, Meta, Glassdoor, Indeed, Google, and similar platforms require an official, licensed, or otherwise permitted access method.

An unofficial connector may be evaluated privately for candidate discovery where the access itself is permitted. For scoring, declare the connector, demonstrate that the access pattern is allowed, and independently verify every published claim with a durable permitted source.

Judge source changes by the amount of useful, attributable evidence they add and whether refresh behavior is reproducible. Requests made, pages fetched, and raw posts collected are operational counts.
